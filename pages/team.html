The Team

Meet the bonded, insured, and certified professionals dedicated to your pet's care.

Evelyn Chen

Founder & Cuddles Captain

Evelyn founded Evelyn The Sitter with a deep passion for providing loving, personalized pet care. With a background in organizational psychology and animal behavioral science, she brings both compassion and expertise to every interaction.

As a lifelong pet advocate and owner of 4 rescue cats and 2 dogs, Evelyn understands the bond between pets and their families. She's dedicated to ensuring every pet receives the best possible care while their owners are away.

Credentials:

  • ✓ Bonded & Insured through Pet Sitter's Associates
  • ✓ Animal Red Cross CPR Certified
  • ✓ PetTech PetSaver Certified
  • ✓ 10+ years animal care experience

Marcus Thompson

Lead Trainer & Senior Sitter

With over 10 years of professional pet care experience, Marcus specializes in behavior training and caring for anxious or special-needs pets. His marathon-running background means he brings energy and enthusiasm to every adventure visit.

Marcus has worked with hundreds of pets and their families, developing expertise in building trust with nervous animals and creating enriching experiences for active dogs.

Credentials:

  • ✓ Professional Pet Sitter (ASPCA Certified)
  • ✓ Animal Red Cross CPR Certified
  • ✓ Anxiety-Focused Care Specialist
  • ✓ 10+ years professional pet care

Priya Patel

Cat Specialist & Operations Lead

Priya's background as a veterinary assistant gives her deep expertise in feline health, behavior, and care. She manages our scheduling and client relations, ensuring every visit is perfectly timed and every client communication is responsive and professional.

Multilingual and detail-oriented, Priya is passionate about understanding each cat's unique personality and needs. Her veterinary background means she can handle medical issues with confidence and care.

Credentials:

  • ✓ Bonded & Insured
  • ✓ Veterinary Assistant (5 years)
  • ✓ PetTech Certified
  • ✓ Feline Behavior Specialist

Jordan Martinez

Adventure Guide & Community Lead

Jordan is an outdoor enthusiast with expertise in enrichment activities and adventure planning. They lead our community outreach programs and specialize in creating engaging, stimulating experiences for dogs of all energy levels.

Fluent in Spanish, Jordan brings warmth and energy to every interaction. Whether it's a hike to a local trail, a park adventure, or just an exciting walk with new friends, Jordan knows how to keep pets engaged and happy.
