FAQs & Policies

Common questions and policies for Evelyn The Sitter services.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Contact us to schedule a free 30-minute meet and greet. This allows us to meet your pets, learn their personalities and routines, and discuss your care needs.

Are your team members bonded and insured?

Yes! All team members are bonded and insured through Pet Sitter's Associates. This protects both you and your pets.

Do you do overnight sitting?

Yes, we offer overnight stays for vacation packages. We can stay at your home from 9pm to 7am. For more information, contact us about custom arrangements.

What if my pet has special medical needs?

We can administer insulin, subcutaneous fluids, and provide specialized palliative care. Contact us to discuss your pet's specific needs and we'll create a customized care plan.

How do I pay for services?

Payments are managed through our Time To Pet client portal. We accept all major payment methods for convenient billing and tracking.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Last-minute cancellations may be subject to a service fee. We're flexible and work to accommodate schedule changes when possible.

Do you care for specialty animals?

Yes! We care for rabbits, birds, bearded dragons, chickens, and other specialty pets. Contact us for a custom quote based on your animal's specific needs.

What if I need emergency care?

We provide emergency services for existing clients. Contact us for details on availability and pricing for emergency pet care.

Can I track my pet's visit?

Yes! Time To Pet provides real-time updates, photos, and notes from each visit. You'll receive notifications before and after every visit.

Service Policies

New Client Requirements

  • 30-minute complimentary meet and greet before first service
  • Completion of client information and pet profile form
  • Emergency contact information required
  • Authorization for photo/video updates

Vacation Package Requirements

  • Two acclimation test visits required prior to owner departure
  • Allows pet to adjust and us to refine care routines
  • Builds confidence and trust before extended visits

Pricing & Billing

  • Base rates shown on our services page
  • Additional fees apply for time variations, multiple pets, specialty care
  • All pricing discussed and approved before services begin
  • Invoice provided after each visit via Time To Pet

Communication & Updates

  • We respond to client inquiries within 24 hours
  • Time To Pet portal provides real-time visit updates
  • Photos and detailed notes sent after each visit
  • Emergency contact number provided to all clients

Safety & Care Standards

  • All team members are bonded and insured
  • Certifications: CPR, PetTech, Professional Pet Sitter status (where applicable)
  • Regular continuing education and training
  • Insurance covers liability and care incidents
