About Evelyn

Evelyn founded Evelyn The Sitter with a passion for providing loving, personalized pet care. With a background in organizational psychology and animal behavioral science, Evelyn brings both compassion and expertise to every pet interaction.

Her philosophy centers on the belief that every pet deserves a guardian who understands their unique personality. As a lifelong pet advocate and owner of 4 rescue cats and 2 dogs, Evelyn has dedicated herself to ensuring pets receive the best care while their families are away.

Evelyn is bonded and insured through Pet Sitter's Associates, holds Animal Red Cross CPR certification, and is a PetTech PetSaver certified professional. She leads a dedicated team committed to the highest standards of pet care.
