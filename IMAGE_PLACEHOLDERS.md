## Recommended Sources

### Free Public Domain / Creative Commons
1. **Unsplash** (https://unsplash.com)
   - Completely free, high-quality images
   - Search: "dog walking", "cat care", "pet sitter", "pet professional"

2. **Pexels** (https://www.pexels.com)
   - Free stock photos
   - Great for pet and lifestyle images

3. **Pixabay** (https://pixabay.com)
   - Free images and videos
   - Search for pet care, animal care topics
